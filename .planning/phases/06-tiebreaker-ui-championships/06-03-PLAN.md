## Phase Goal

> **ROADMAP goal (verbatim):** Users can see exactly how each conference's championship matchup was determined, see a fully ranked 1..N conference table wherever the tiebreaker procedure can determine an order, and resolve any tie the engine can't settle on its own.

**This plan's slice.** This is the flagship user-visible change of the phase. Before it, a user opening the standings sidebar sees clumps of teams sharing rank numbers — and, on about 1% of tables, sees `1 Boston College 6-2` sitting above `1 Duke 7-2`. After it, the SEC, Big Ten and Big 12 show a clean 1 through N, and the ACC shows every rank its one computable step can determine, with the genuinely-tied teams still grouped.

<objective>
Replace the standings layer's own ranking machinery with the engine's ordered partition, and put the whole computation behind one composable.

D-01 reverses Phase 5's D-04: standings show distinct ranks 1..N rather than shared ranks. 06-RESEARCH.md Pattern 5 is the shape of the change — `rankComponents` is a union-find over the equivalence closure of "same seed group" and "identical conference W-L", and D-01 plus D-04's reversal remove both relations' reason to exist. The engine now emits the partition directly and in order, so `computeStandings` **deletes** its ranking code rather than extending it.

Folded in because this plan is already changing these exact types:
- **WR-06** (deferred from Phase 5): tighten `StandingsResult` so "every P4 conference is always present" is expressed in the type.
- **IN-02** (deferred from Phase 5): extract the week page's standings/tiebreaker orchestration into `useStandings`, collapsing two divergent readiness guards and a duplicated `toOutcomes` derivation into one seam.

Purpose: TIE-08's first half — ranked 1..N by iteratively applying the published procedure, with genuinely-unseparable teams sharing a rank.
Output: a simplified `computeStandings`, a tightened `StandingsResult`, and `app/composables/useStandings.ts`.
</objective>

## Conflict resolved by this plan: the degraded fallback path

`resolveAllConferences` catches a per-conference throw and omits that conference; `computeStandings` then ranks by record alone. 06-RESEARCH.md Open Question 4 and 06-UI-SPEC.md §10 both say the degraded path must still produce a rank sequence and must mark nothing as tiebreaker-decided. 06-UI-SPEC.md §10 additionally says "every team is its own rank group", which would give two teams with an identical conference record different rank numbers — a rank gap that ROADMAP criterion 6 says must never be mistakable for a record gap.

**Resolution, binding:** on the degraded path, teams sharing an identical conference W-L share one rank group and carry `isTied: true`; every other team is its own group. Nothing on that path is marked tiebreaker-decided, which is UI-SPEC §10's actual concern — and it is satisfied structurally, because `StandingsTable` reads its markers from the `ranking` prop, which is `undefined` for an omitted conference, so no chip, pill or band can render regardless. This grouping is a single `groupBy` over (wins, losses) on the already-sorted list, not a reintroduced closure: there are no seed groups to close over when the conference was omitted.

This path should be unreachable after Plans 01 and 02 (measured 44/400 guard trips before, 0/400 after). It is retained because the try/catch is retained.
