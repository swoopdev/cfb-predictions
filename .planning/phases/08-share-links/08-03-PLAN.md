## Phase Goal

> **ROADMAP goal (verbatim):** Users can share a scenario via a URL that opens with those exact picks applied, without ever clobbering the visitor's own picks.

**Planner note (MVP mode):** see Plan 08-01's Phase Goal section for the full discrepancy note on this ROADMAP line's format and the synthesized user-story equivalent; not repeated here.

**This plan's slice.** Everything the phase promised becomes real. A visitor can open a share link and immediately see the shared scenario's picks and standings without logging in or losing anything of their own; a schedule mismatch degrades to an honest partial-apply count instead of silent corruption; a corrupted link degrades to a clear "couldn't be read" message instead of a crash; and "Save a copy" turns a temporary preview into a permanent, ordinary scenario in one click.
